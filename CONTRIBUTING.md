# Contributing

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

欢迎贡献。

提交 Issue、建议或 Pull Request，即表示你同意：

- 你的贡献可在本仓库中按 `GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later` 发布
- 你有权提交该贡献
- 你同时授予项目维护者对该贡献进行再授权的权利，包括用于商业授权、双授权或其他分发安排

如果你不同意上述条款，请不要提交代码贡献。

## English

Contributions are welcome.

By submitting an issue, suggestion, or pull request, you agree that:

- your contribution may be distributed in this repository under `GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later`
- you have the right to submit the contribution
- you also grant the project maintainer the right to relicense that contribution for commercial licensing, dual licensing, or other distribution arrangements

If you do not agree to these terms, please do not submit code contributions.
